Flagship Program

National Convention

The SHPE National Convention is the largest gathering of Hispanic STEM professionals in the country. Every fall, thousands of students, engineers, scientists, and executives come together for a week of career fairs, technical workshops, leadership sessions, and cultural celebrations.

SHPE Columbia sponsors members to attend, supporting travel and registration so that cost is never a barrier to this transformative experience. Application coming soon.

Women in STEM

SHPEtinas

SHPEtinas is a dedicated space within SHPE Columbia that centers and celebrates Latina women in engineering. Through mentorship programming, skill-building workshops, and a tight-knit sisterhood, SHPEtinas empowers its members to lead with confidence in technical fields.

Whether you're navigating your first internship search or advocating for yourself in a male-dominated space, SHPEtinas has your back — every step of the way.

Día de Ciencias

Día de Ciencias ("Day of Science") is SHPE Columbia's outreach program, partnering with local New York City high schools to bring STEM to life for the next generation of Latino engineers.

SHPE members design and facilitate hands-on science and engineering workshops — from circuits to robotics — showing young students from underrepresented communities that STEM is for them.

Academic Support

MentorSHPE Program

MentorSHPE pairs underclassmen with upperclassmen for one-on-one mentorship throughout the academic year. Mentors provide guidance on coursework, research opportunities, internship applications, and navigating life as a Hispanic engineer at Columbia.

Mentees gain access to a large network of upperclassmen who have exceptional experiences. Mentors deepen their leadership and giving-back skills. Everyone grows.

Café con Leche

Café con Leche is a networking event hosted every Spring. It brings together New York City professionals from all industries — finance, tech, consulting, and beyond — with Columbia students in a relaxed, conversational setting.

Hosted in collaboration with LPEN (Latino Professional and Executive Network), this event creates authentic connections over coffee, bridging the gap between campus and career in true NYC fashion.

Community Building

SHPE Chapter Mixers

The SHPE Chapter Mixers bring together SHPE chapters from across New York City to connect, share resources, and build a unified community. Chapters from Columbia, NYU, and beyond come together to strengthen the larger SHPE NYC ecosystem.

These mixers foster resilience, cross-chapter support, and a sense of belonging that extends beyond any single campus — reminding us that our familia is bigger than we think.
