Why Partner With SHPE Columbia

Reach Exceptional Hispanic Talent

SHPE Columbia University is the premier professional organization for Hispanic engineers at one of the world's top research universities. Our members are Columbia Engineering students spanning computer science, biomedical, civil, electrical, industrial, and mechanical engineering — actively seeking internships, full-time roles, and meaningful industry connections.

A sponsorship with SHPE Columbia is not just a brand placement — it's a direct pipeline to a community that is ambitious, diverse, and ready to make an impact in your organization.
